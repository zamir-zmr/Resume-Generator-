Privacy Policy – Resume Maker

ZmrAppStudio · Effective date: November 10, 2026

ZmrAppStudio built Resume Maker (also called CV Builder) as a free tool that lets you write, edit, design, save, share and download your own resume / Curriculum Vitae (CV). This page explains what the app does, what information is handled, and how your privacy is protected. By using the app you agree to this policy.

About the App (WebView Container)

Resume Maker is a WebView container: it displays web content inside the app. The page it opens is set by us through a link stored in Firebase, so we can update the resume builder without releasing a new version. If no link is set, the app opens the built-in resume builder that ships with the app. Because the app loads this content from the internet, an internet connection may be used while the app opens. We choose the content that is loaded and do not load pages that collect personal information from you.

Information You Enter (Your Resume Data)

To create a resume you may type details such as your name, email address, phone number, address, date of birth, education, work experience, skills, languages, hobbies and other personal details, and you may add a photo. All of this information stays on your device. It is stored only in your device’s local browser/WebView storage so that your resume is still there when you open the app again. We do not upload, collect, receive, store on our servers, sell or share your resume data or photo. If you clear the app data or uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device.

Photo

If you tap “Photo”, you choose a picture from your device (gallery or camera, depending on your device). The photo is used only inside your resume on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any server.

PDF, Print, Save, Share and Load

  • PDF / Print: your resume is converted to a PDF file or sent to your device’s print service on your device. When you generate a PDF for the first time the app may download the open-source libraries html2canvas and jsPDF from the cdnjs content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com); this is a normal file download and no resume data is sent. The PDF is created on your device and saved to your device’s downloads/storage.
  • Save: stores your resume on your device and downloads a backup file (.json) to your device.
  • Share: opens your device’s share sheet so you can send your resume backup file or data through an app of your choice (for example WhatsApp, email or Drive). What you share and with whom is fully your choice and is controlled by the app you pick; we do not receive it. Please share your resume only with people you trust, because it may contain personal details.
  • Load: lets you pick a backup file from your device to restore a resume. The file is read only on your device.

Permissions

The app may ask for access to your photos/files (to choose a photo or backup file, or to save a PDF) and the camera (only if your device offers camera capture when adding a photo). These are used only for the features above. The app does not access your contacts, location, microphone, SMS or call logs.

Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase services: Firebase Realtime Database (to read the link of the content to display) and Firebase Analytics. Firebase may automatically collect limited technical and usage data such as app launches, app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate region (from IP address), crash and diagnostic reports, and an app-instance identifier. This data is used only to keep the app working, fix problems and understand overall usage. It is not used for advertising, profiling or personalised content, and it does not include your resume content. Firebase is operated by Google; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Account / Sign-in

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask you to create an account.

Advertising

The app does not show ads. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children’s Privacy

Resume Maker is intended for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all resume data stays on your device, we hold no personal data to delete; if you believe a child’s information has been sent to us, contact us and we will take the necessary action.

Cookies and Local Storage

The app does not use advertising cookies. It uses local storage on your device to remember your resume, layout, theme, font and the last opened link.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your resume data lives on your device until you press Reset, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Files you download (PDF, backup .json) or share are saved where you or the receiving app save them; please delete them yourself if you no longer need them. Since we do not collect your resume data, we cannot access or delete it for you.

Security

We use commercially reasonable means to protect the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Keep your downloaded PDF and backup files in a safe place.

Links to Other Sites

The app may open external pages (for example Google Play or the apps you choose when sharing). We do not control those sites or apps; please read their privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.
