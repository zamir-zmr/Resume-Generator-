Privacy Policy – Resume Maker

ZmrAppStudio · Effective date: November 9, 2026

ZmrAppStudio built Resume Maker, a free app that helps you create, edit, print, save, share and export your CV (resume) as a PDF. This page explains what information the app handles and how. By using the app you agree to this policy.

About the App (WebView Container)

Resume Maker is a WebView container: it displays web content inside the app. The page it opens is set by us through a link stored in Firebase, so we can update the app without releasing a new version. If no link is set, the app opens the built-in CV editor that ships with the app. Because the app loads this content from the internet, an internet connection may be used when the app opens.

Information You Enter

To build your CV you may type details such as your name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, education, work experience, skills and other text, and you may add a photo. This information stays on your device. It is stored locally in the app’s on-device storage so your CV is still there next time. We do not upload your CV details or photo to our servers, we cannot see them, and we do not sell or share them. You can erase them any time with the Reset button, by clearing the app’s storage in your phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Photo

When you tap Photo, the app opens your device’s file or gallery picker so you choose an image. The app does not access your photos or camera in any other way, and the chosen photo is used only inside your CV on your device.

PDF, Print, Save, Load and Share

PDF: your CV is converted to a PDF on your device. The PDF is saved to or opened from a location you choose and is not sent to us. Print: uses your device’s print service. Save / Load: creates or reads a CV file (JSON) that you choose. Share: opens your device’s share sheet so you can send your CV file or PDF to any app or person you pick (for example WhatsApp, email or Drive). Sharing happens only when you tap Share, and what is shared is handled by the app you choose under that app’s own privacy policy. Please share your CV only with people and services you trust, because it may contain personal details.

Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase: Realtime Database (to read the link of the content to display) and Firebase Analytics. Firebase may automatically collect limited technical and usage data such as app launches, app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate region (from IP address), crash and diagnostic reports, and an app-instance identifier. We use this only to keep the app working, fix problems and understand overall usage. It does not include the CV details you type. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-Party Libraries

To create PDFs, the editor loads open-source libraries (jsPDF and html2canvas) from the cdnjs content delivery network (Cloudflare). Like any website request, this lets the CDN see your IP address and device information. Your CV content is not sent to the CDN.

Advertising

The app does not show ads and does not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children’s Privacy

Resume Maker is intended for job seekers and general users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take the necessary action.

Cookies and Local Storage

The app does not use advertising cookies. It uses local storage on your device to remember your CV, layout, theme and font choices.

Security

Your CV data stays on your device, so its safety also depends on your device’s security (screen lock, backups). We use commercially reasonable means to protect the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Links to Other Sites

The app may open external pages or share targets that we do not control. Please read their privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.
